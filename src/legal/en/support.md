# Support

Last updated: 27 June 2026

## Contacts

- General support: support@trtkat.cz
- Privacy/GDPR: privacy@trtkat.cz
- App security incidents: security@trtkat.cz
- Community and child safety: safety@trtkat.cz
- DSA contact point: dsa@trtkat.cz
- Store review contact: review@trtkat.cz
- Website: https://trtkat.cz
- App: https://app.trtkat.cz

We do not publish a public phone number.

## What we can help with

- login and account recovery,
- account deletion,
- reporting users or content,
- blocking and safety settings,
- suspected fake profiles,
- suspected underage users,
- GDPR requests,
- app technical issues.

## Response times

Target response times:

- general support: 2 to 5 business days,
- rule violation reports: within 48 hours,
- harassment, threats, stalking or blackmail: within 24 hours,
- suspected underage user, CSEA/CSAM or immediate danger: highest priority,
- app security vulnerability: acknowledgement within 24 hours.

These are target times and may vary depending on volume and severity.

## What to include in your request

Include:

- account email,
- brief description of the problem,
- date and time of incident if known,
- profile or user involved,
- screenshots only if they do not contain unnecessary sensitive data.

Never send your password.

## Store review

For Apple/Google review use review@trtkat.cz and prepare a separate demo account. Do not publish demo passwords in public documents.
