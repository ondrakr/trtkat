# Account Deletion

Last updated: 27 June 2026

This page describes how to delete your Trtkat account and what happens to your data.

## 1. How to delete your account

You can delete your account:

- in the app: Account / Settings / Delete account,
- through the web form on this page,
- by email: privacy@trtkat.cz.

Apple and Google require apps with accounts to allow in-app account deletion. The web form is mainly for Google Play and users who no longer have app access.

## 2. Request verification

To prevent unauthorized deletion we may ask for identity verification as needed, for example:

- logging into the account,
- confirmation via the account email,
- additional checks if the request comes from a different email.

We never ask for your password by email.

## 3. What we delete or anonymize

After confirmed deletion we remove or anonymize especially:

- profile,
- email and login credentials,
- date of birth,
- profile photos,
- bio, interests and preferences,
- visibility settings,
- swipes, likes and matches,
- regular chat messages according to final product implementation,
- push tokens,
- regular analytics linkage to the account.

Recommended product approach: replace identity in existing conversations with a neutral label such as "Deleted user" or delete/anonymize messages so they are no longer linked to the original account.

## 4. What we may temporarily retain

Some data may be retained in limited scope if necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal protection or legal obligations:

- security logs: 6 months,
- reports and blocks: 12 months,
- serious reports, threats, fraud, CSEA/CSAM or legal disputes: up to 24 months or as legally required,
- backups: 30 to 90 days depending on backup rotation,
- record of account deletion: limited proof of request handling.

Temporary retention is not for normal account use and data will not be shown to other users.

## 5. Timeline

We typically delete or anonymize within 30 days of verifying the request. Complex cases may take longer under GDPR, especially if the request is part of a security incident, report or legal obligation.

## 6. Hiding is not deletion

"Hide profile" or "do not show to others" only limits visibility. It does not delete the account or remove personal data.

## 7. Payments

The current version has no payments or subscriptions.
