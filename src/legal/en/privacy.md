## 2. Who the app is for

Trtkat is a dating and social app for adults in the EU. The app is only for users aged 18 and older.

When registering, users confirm they are at least 18 and provide their date of birth. We use date of birth to verify the age limit. Other users see age, not date of birth.

If we discover or reasonably suspect that an account is used by someone under 18, we may temporarily hide, restrict, request additional verification, or delete the account.

## 3. Data we process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

### Account and login

- email,
- secure password hash,
- data from Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In if you use those methods,
- user ID,
- account creation date,
- date of birth for 18+ verification,
- email verification,
- password reset and account security events.

We currently do not require or use phone numbers for registration.

### Profile

- first name or display name,
- age,
- gender,
- who you want to meet,
- voluntarily entered preferences,
- bio, interests, height and availability,
- profile photos,
- profile visibility settings.

We do not show other users your email, phone number, or exact date of birth.

### Photos and media

You may upload up to 9 profile photos. We remove technical metadata, especially EXIF data, where technically possible. Photos may be checked automatically and/or manually if reported or for security reasons.

### App usage

- swipes, likes, passes and matches,
- messages and chat threads,
- photos, links, voice messages or video in chat if enabled,
- blocks, unmatches and reports,
- privacy and notification settings,
- interactions with app features.

Messages are not public. They may be accessed by the operator only to the extent necessary, for example when reported, during a security incident, suspected rule violation, or legal obligation.

### Location

- precise GPS location if you grant device permission,
- manually entered city or location,
- calculated distance between users.

Precise location is used for nearby recommendations and distance filtering. We do not show exact coordinates to other users. The recommended implementation is to display only rounded distance or an approximate area.

We do not store location history unless explicitly added to the app and these policies.

### Technical and security data

- IP address,
- device type, operating system and app version,
- app instance ID or device ID for security and diagnostics,
- request date and time,
- security logs,
- crash logs and diagnostics,
- push tokens for notifications,
- data needed to protect against spam, fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.

We do not use advertising identifiers for ads unless explicitly added and consented to.

### Analytics

We may process basic analytics events such as registration, onboarding completion, feature usage, errors and app stability. Analytics must not contain private message content, precise GPS coordinates, or sensitive profile text.

If analytics requiring consent is used, we will provide the user with an appropriate choice.

### Payments

The current version of the app has no payments, subscriptions, in-app purchases or advertising. We do not process payment data.

## 4. Sensitive data

Trtkat is an adults-only dating app. Some data users voluntarily provide may reveal information about private life, sexual orientation, preferences or other sensitive circumstances.

Sensitive preference fields should be optional, clearly voluntary, editable and removable by the user, and not used for advertising or shared with third parties for marketing.

## 5. Why we process data

We process data to:

- create and manage accounts,
- verify that users are 18 or older,
- provide profiles, discovery, likes, matches and chat,
- show nearby users and distance filters,
- send notifications,
- provide support,
- detect spam, fraud, abuse and safety risks,
- moderate content and handle reports,
- comply with legal obligations,
- diagnose crashes and improve app stability,
- measure product usage where permitted.

Legal bases include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent where required, and legal obligations where applicable.

## 6. Who we share data with

We may share or provide access to data to:

- hosting and infrastructure providers,
- database and photo storage providers,
- email, push notification, analytics or crash reporting providers,
- Apple and Google sign-in providers,
- moderators or service administrators,
- legal, security or technical advisors,
- public authorities if required by law.

## 7. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Trtkat is intended for EU users. We prefer providers storing data in the EU/EEA. Some providers may process technical or support data outside the EU/EEA using GDPR mechanisms such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

## 8. How long we retain data

- account and profile: while the account exists,
- date of birth: while the account exists for 18+ verification,
- profile photos: while the account exists or until deleted by the user,
- swipes, likes and matches: while the account exists or until deleted/anonymized,
- regular messages: while the account exists or until deleted/anonymized,
- security logs: 6 months,
- crash logs: 90 days,
- analytics: up to 14 months,
- reports and blocks: 12 months; serious cases up to 24 months,
- backups: typically 30 to 90 days,
- inactive accounts: notice after 24 months of inactivity, then hide and delete/anonymize if no response.

## 9. Account deletion

Users can delete their account:

- in the app: Account / Settings / Delete account,
- on the web: https://trtkat.cz/account-deletion,
- by email: privacy@trtkat.cz.

After account deletion, we delete or anonymize personal data linked to the account within 30 days unless limited retention is required for legal, security or legitimate reasons.

Temporary deactivation or hiding a profile is not the same as account deletion.

## 10. User rights under GDPR

Users have the right to:

- access personal data,
- rectification of inaccurate data,
- erasure,
- restriction of processing,
- data portability,
- object to processing based on legitimate interests,
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent,
- lodge a complaint with the Czech Data Protection Authority.

Contact for exercising rights: privacy@trtkat.cz. We typically respond within 30 days.

## 11. Security

We use technical and organizational measures including HTTPS/TLS, password hashing with Argon2id or bcrypt, email verification, secure password reset, access restrictions, admin MFA, rate limiting, security event logging, private photo storage, signed access URLs, EXIF removal, and regular backups.

Security reports: security@trtkat.cz.

## 12. Children and minors

Trtkat is only for persons over 18. Content sexualizing minors, child exploitation, grooming, CSAM/CSEA or attempts to contact minors are prohibited.

Report suspicions in the app or to safety@trtkat.cz. Such reports are handled with highest priority.

## 13. Automated decision-making

We may use automated systems to detect spam, fraud, inappropriate photos, security risks or community rule violations. Automated outputs may lead to content visibility restrictions, manual review flags, or temporary account restrictions. Manual review is recommended for serious account actions.

## 14. Policy changes

We may update these policies. We will inform users of material changes through the app or by email.
