# Community Guidelines

Last updated: 27 June 2026

Trtkat is a dating and social app for adults 18+. The goal is to enable new contacts, friendship and casual meetups safely, voluntarily and with respect.

## 1. Basic rule

Treat others so they can feel safe in the app. Consent, respect and truthful identity are essential.

## 2. Adults 18+ only

Trtkat is only for persons over 18.

Prohibited:

- creating an account if you are under 18,
- providing a false age or date of birth,
- helping a minor use the app,
- sharing, seeking or creating sexualized content involving minors,
- contacting minors for sexual or intimate behavior.

Report suspected underage users or child-safety issues in the app or to safety@trtkat.cz.

## 3. Profile and photos

Your profile should represent you.

Allowed:

- your own current photos,
- appropriate profile photos,
- truthful basic information,
- bio and interests without insults, spam or illegal offers.

Prohibited:

- photos of others without their consent,
- impersonation,
- fake profiles,
- nudity and explicit sexual content in profile photos,
- pornography,
- photos of minors in inappropriate contexts,
- photos containing others' private data,
- content violating copyright or likeness rights.

By uploading a photo you confirm you have the right to use it.

## 4. Chat and messages

Prohibited in chat:

- harassment,
- threats,
- stalking,
- coercion,
- blackmail,
- unsolicited explicit messages,
- sending intimate content without clear recipient consent,
- spam and mass messages,
- phishing, fraud and dangerous links,
- requests for money, investments or financial help from strangers,
- sharing others' personal data without consent.

Recommended: first messages without a match should work as message requests. Recipients should be able to accept, reject, report or block the sender.

## 5. Sexual content and paid services

Trtkat may serve adult dating but is not intended for pornography or commercial sex.

Prohibited:

- pornography,
- public nudity,
- offers or requests for paid sexual services,
- escort, trafficking or prostitution facilitation,
- pressure for intimate meetings,
- intimate content blackmail,
- sharing intimate content without consent.

## 6. Hate, violence and harmful content

Prohibited:

- hate speech,
- discrimination,
- violent threats,
- support for self-harm or suicide,
- bullying,
- humiliation,
- illegal drugs or illegal weapons,
- content promoting criminal activity.

## 7. Safety, spam and fraud

Prohibited:

- spam,
- automated bots,
- profile scraping,
- phishing,
- malware,
- fake contests,
- investment or cryptocurrency scams,
- bypassing app limits,
- bypassing blocks or moderation.

## 8. Report and block

Every user must be able to:

- report a profile,
- report a photo,
- report a message or chat,
- block a user,
- unmatch,
- contact support.

After block or unmatch, the user should disappear from normal view and should not be able to continue communication. Reports should remain available for a reasonable time after unmatch.

## 9. How we handle violations

Depending on severity we may:

- hide content,
- remove photos or profile text,
- temporarily restrict accounts,
- disable chat or profile visibility,
- block accounts,
- permanently delete accounts,
- retain limited evidence for safety or legal purposes,
- escalate serious cases to authorities where required by law.

Target response times:

- regular reports: within 48 hours,
- harassment, threats, stalking, blackmail or suspected underage account: within 24 hours,
- CSAM/CSEA or immediate danger: highest priority.

## 10. Appeals and review

If you believe we made a mistake, contact support@trtkat.cz with your account email and a brief description.

Serious safety restrictions may remain in place during review.

## 11. Safe dating

We recommend:

- not sharing address, workplace, ID numbers, documents or financial data with strangers,
- not sending money,
- planning first meetings in public places,
- telling someone close where you are going,
- ending contact when pressured or when behavior feels wrong,
- using report and block.

## 12. Contacts

Support: support@trtkat.cz

Community and child safety: safety@trtkat.cz

App security incidents: security@trtkat.cz
