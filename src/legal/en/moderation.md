# Moderation Policy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

This document describes how Trtkat handles user content, reports, blocks and account actions.

## 1. What we moderate

We may moderate:

- profile photos,
- bio and profile text,
- name or display name,
- chat messages and media when reported or for security reasons,
- links,
- user behavior,
- reports, blocks and suspicious usage patterns.

## 2. Basic principle

Profile photos are checked automatically before or immediately after upload. Manual review occurs mainly for reports, risky cases and appeals.

We do not routinely read private messages without reason. Messages may be reviewed when reported, during a security incident, suspected abuse or legal obligation.

## 3. Reports

Users must be able to report:

- profiles,
- photos,
- messages,
- chats,
- suspected underage users,
- spam/fraud,
- harassment,
- illegal or dangerous content.

Reports should remain available for a reasonable time after unmatch or block.

## 4. Response times

Target response times:

- regular report: within 48 hours,
- harassment, threats, stalking, blackmail, fraud: within 24 hours,
- suspected underage user: within 24 hours,
- CSAM/CSEA or immediate danger: highest priority.

## 5. Possible actions

Depending on severity we may:

- reject or remove a photo,
- remove profile text,
- restrict profile visibility,
- temporarily hide an account,
- disable chat or certain features,
- block a user,
- permanently delete an account,
- retain limited evidence for safety and legal purposes,
- escalate to authorities where required by law.

## 6. Automated moderation

Automated systems may flag or block:

- nudity and explicit content,
- suspected minors,
- spam,
- phishing,
- dangerous links,
- repeated copied messages,
- mass first messages,
- suspicious accounts.

Manual review should be available for serious actions.

## 7. Appeals

Users may request review of moderation decisions via support@trtkat.cz with account email and a brief explanation.

Serious safety restrictions may remain during review.

## 8. DSA notice-and-action

For the EU we recommend a simple notice-and-action process:

- receive reports via app or dsa@trtkat.cz,
- acknowledge receipt,
- assess the report,
- decide and act,
- inform affected parties where appropriate,
- allow request for review.

## 9. Contacts

Support: support@trtkat.cz
