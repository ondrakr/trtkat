# Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 27 June 2026

Trtkat is an app for adults 18+ only. Google Play and Apple may still require public child safety standards because the app enables profiles, photos, chat and user communication.

## 1. Zero tolerance

Trtkat has zero tolerance for:

- child sexual abuse and exploitation,
- CSAM/CSEA content,
- grooming,
- sexualization of minors,
- attempts to contact minors for sexual purposes,
- sharing, offering, requesting or linking to CSAM/CSEA.

## 2. Age limit

The app is only for persons over 18.

During registration users:

- confirm 18+,
- enter date of birth,
- cannot complete registration if they do not reach 18 based on date of birth.

Additional age or identity verification may be added in the future for risky accounts, reports or suspected underage users.

## 3. How to report child-safety concerns

Users can report suspicions:

- in the app via profile, photo or message report,
- by email to safety@trtkat.cz.

Include:

- who is involved,
- what happened,
- when it happened,
- screenshot or evidence only if it can be shared safely.

Do not forward explicit content unless necessary. If there is immediate danger, also contact local authorities.

## 4. What we do when we receive a report

When we suspect an underage account or CSEA/CSAM we may:

- immediately hide or restrict the account,
- block chat,
- remove or disable content,
- preserve limited evidence as necessary,
- perform manual review,
- request additional verification,
- delete the account,
- escalate to authorities where required by law.

Target response time for child-safety reports is within 24 hours; CSAM/CSEA or immediate danger has highest priority.

## 5. Moderation and prevention

Recommended for first release:

- automatic profile photo review before publication,
- profile, photo and message reports,
- user blocking,
- manual review of risky reports,
- limits against spam and mass contact,
- report access after unmatch,
- prohibition of public nudity and explicit content,
- prohibition of others' photos without consent.

## 6. Cooperation with authorities

For CSAM/CSEA suspicions we follow applicable law in the Czech Republic and EU. The exact reporting process must be confirmed by legal counsel before launch.
